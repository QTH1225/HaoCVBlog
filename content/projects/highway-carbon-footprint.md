## Summary

- Modeled and assessed cradle-to-gate emissions and explored strategies for lower-carbon highway construction.

## PROJECT OVERVIEW

Led a study of the cradle-to-gate carbon footprint of Anqing Expressway's S1 construction section. The project combined emissions modeling, assessment, and optimization strategies in the context of China's carbon peaking and carbon neutrality goals.
