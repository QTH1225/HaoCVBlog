## Summary

- Studied precast frame–shear wall design and durability using ICCP technology and TRC formwork; awarded Outstanding Undergraduate Thesis.

## PROJECT OVERVIEW

Used the Hongdong Fishery and Tourism Hotel in Xiamen as a case study for optimizing a precast frame–shear wall structure. The thesis examined impressed current cathodic protection (ICCP) and textile-reinforced concrete (TRC) formwork in structural design and durability analysis.

Recognized as an Outstanding Undergraduate Thesis at China University of Mining and Technology in June 2025.
