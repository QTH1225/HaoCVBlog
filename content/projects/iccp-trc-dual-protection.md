## Summary

- Led a nationally funded undergraduate project combining ICCP and permanent TRC formwork for structural protection.

## PROJECT OVERVIEW

Investigated a structural system combining impressed current cathodic protection (ICCP) with textile-reinforced concrete (TRC) permanent formwork. The national undergraduate innovation project received RMB 15,000 in funding and explored the two approaches as a combined protection strategy.
